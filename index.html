Numerical checks & interpretation

This is an exploratory finite-resolution simulation. A negative mean energy flux supports inverse transfer; a broad inertial range or a −5/3 law needs longer runs, resolution and timestep comparisons, and uncertainty estimates.

Method and normalization

Fourier pseudospectral solver with strict 2/3 dealiasing. Exact stochastic linear half-steps surround an RK4 nonlinear step. Independent Gaussian kicks have fixed covariance and mean energy injection ε. Initial velocity and vorticity are zero. For spatial drag, the force is −α(x)u: its full curl enters the vorticity equation, and mean velocity is evolved. Drag products use an exact Fourier step profile and a padded Galerkin projection; the plot still shows the full vorticity grid.

E = ½⟨|u|²⟩, Z = ½⟨ω²⟩. E(k) includes every mode in each radial shell, including the multiplicity of the real FFT. Π(K) = −Σ|q|<KRe[ω̂* N̂]/q². Plots stop inside the complete dealiased disk.

Budget residuals compare final invariants with accumulated stochastic work and damping increments. Instantaneous nonlinear energy and enstrophy transfers sum to zero up to roundoff. The vorticity plot uses the full numerical grid without additional filtering.
